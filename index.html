Al Ahsa Crash Risk

Geospatial machine learning · Interactive risk map

ML · Geospatial

Predicts crash likelihood for every road segment in Al Ahsa and renders it as a clickable Leaflet map. Click any road to see its predicted risk; change the weather and every segment re-scores live.

  • Engineered features across 21,331 OSM road segments: curvature ratio, road class, length and weather surrogates.
  • Trained a Gradient Boosting Regressor reaching R² = 0.958 on a synthetic crash history, with the scoring scale derived from the target's 99th percentile.
  • Served predictions through a FastAPI backend feeding a Leaflet map with live weather-scenario overrides (rain, visibility, temperature, condition).
  • Hardened the pipeline: predictions join on segment ID rather than row order, and model artefacts load with a rule-based fallback.

Hospital Voice Assistant

Conversational AI · Bilingual booking system

AI · Voice

A hospital appointment system patients can simply talk to. Speak in Arabic or English and the assistant checks live doctor availability and books the slot, with no forms to fill in.
